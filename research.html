Research

Imaging technology from physics to translation

Our group combines imaging physics, RF engineering, electromagnetic simulation, and computation to develop reproducible tools for biomedical research and clinical imaging.

01

RF Coil Design

We develop and evaluate RF coil technologies for MRI, including coil geometry optimization, transmit and receive performance, sensitivity, and imaging applications.

02

Electromagnetic Simulation

We use computational electromagnetic simulation to investigate RF field distributions, B1 efficiency, electric fields, specific absorption rate, and coil-sample interactions.

03

Magnetic Resonance Imaging

Our MRI research focuses on advanced imaging technology, quantitative imaging, hardware development, and translation of imaging methods to biomedical applications.

04

Medical Imaging Physics

We investigate medical imaging technologies through quantitative image-quality evaluation, physics-based optimization, and development of new imaging methodologies.

05

Quantitative Imaging

We develop quantitative imaging techniques and computational approaches for extracting reproducible imaging biomarkers.

06

Computational Imaging

We develop image reconstruction, denoising, and quantitative image-analysis approaches to improve measurement robustness and imaging performance.

Approach

Build. Measure. Quantify. Translate.

We approach imaging problems across the full technical pipeline: electromagnetic simulation and hardware development, acquisition optimization, image reconstruction, and quantitative analysis.

This integrated workflow connects fundamental imaging physics with practical applications in biomedical and clinical research.
