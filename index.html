Research

Imaging technology from physics to translation

Our group combines imaging physics, engineering, and computation to develop reproducible tools for biomedical research and clinical imaging.

01

Quantitative MRI

Quantitative imaging methods for tissue characterization, reproducible measurements, and imaging biomarkers.

02

Multinuclear & Metabolic MRI

Development of multinuclear MRI and MRS approaches to investigate metabolism, physiology, and disease-related changes.

03

RF Engineering & MR Hardware

RF coil design, electromagnetic simulation, prototyping, and application-specific hardware for advanced MR experiments.

04

Computational Imaging

AI-enabled denoising, reconstruction, and quantitative image analysis designed to improve measurement robustness and imaging performance.

Approach

Build. Measure. Quantify. Translate.

We approach imaging problems across the full technical pipeline: electromagnetic simulation and hardware development, pulse-sequence and acquisition optimization, image reconstruction, and quantitative analysis.

This integrated workflow allows us to connect fundamental imaging physics with practical applications in biomedical and clinical research.
